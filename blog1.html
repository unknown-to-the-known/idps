Campus Life

Life at IDPS PU College

March 18, 2026 By Admin 5 min read

Discover the vibrant campus life at IDPS PU College, Ballari. From state-of-the-art laboratories to lush green gardens, our campus provides the perfect environment for academic excellence and personal growth.

A Campus Built for Learning

Spread across 15 acres of lush green landscape, IDPS PU College offers an environment that inspires learning and creativity. The campus features modern architectural designs that blend seamlessly with nature, creating a peaceful atmosphere conducive to focused study.

State-of-the-Art Laboratories

Our physics, chemistry, and biology laboratories are equipped with the latest instruments and safety equipment. Students get hands-on experience with experiments that reinforce theoretical concepts. The computer science lab features high-performance systems with the latest software for programming and design courses.

"The practical exposure we get in the labs is exceptional. It has helped me understand complex concepts easily and prepared me well for competitive exams like NEET." – Priya S., II PUC Student

Library & Resource Center

The two-story library houses over 12,000 books, including reference materials, competitive exam guides, and digital resources. The reading garden adjacent to the library provides a serene spot for students to study amidst nature. Regular workshops on research skills and digital literacy are conducted here.

Sports and Fitness

Physical fitness is an integral part of our curriculum. The college boasts a full-size football field, basketball and tennis courts, an indoor badminton facility, and a modern gymnasium. Trained coaches guide students in various sports, and inter-house tournaments are held throughout the year.

Cultural Activities and Events

Life at IDPS is not just about academics. The annual cultural fest, freshers' party, sendoff, and various club activities ensure holistic development. Students participate in debates, quizzes, music, dance, and drama, showcasing their talents and building confidence.

Hostel Life

Separate hostel facilities for boys and girls offer a home away from home. Nutritious meals, 24/7 security, Wi-Fi connectivity, and common rooms with recreational activities ensure students feel comfortable and focused on their studies. Wardens and mentors provide guidance and support.

The campus also features a spacious cafeteria serving hygienic and nutritious meals, an amphitheater for outdoor events, and plenty of green spaces for students to relax and interact. At IDPS PU College, every day brings new opportunities for learning, growth, and friendship.
