About IDPS PU College

IDPS PU College, a unit of the International Delhi Public School Society, offers a transformative pre‑university experience. Our lush green campus and distinguished faculty nurture academic excellence, critical thinking, and holistic growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What are the admission criteria?

Admission is based on merit and performance in the entrance test. Students who have completed 10th from a recognized board are eligible.

2. Does the college provide hostel facilities?

Yes, separate hostel facilities for boys and girls are available within the campus with modern amenities and 24/7 security.

3. What competitive exam coaching is available?

Integrated coaching for NEET, JEE, KCET, and other national-level entrance tests with experienced faculty and regular mock tests.

4. What is the fee structure?

The fee structure is affordable and competitive. Contact our admissions office or visit the college website for detailed information.

5. What transportation facilities are available?

A fleet of buses covers major routes across the city, ensuring safe and convenient transportation for day scholars.

6. What makes the teaching staff special?

Our faculty comprises experienced, passionate, and result-driven educators who provide personalized attention and mentorship.
